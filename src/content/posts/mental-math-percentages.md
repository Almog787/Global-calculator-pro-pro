# Mental Math: Percentages Made Easy

Ever stood in a store trying to figure out what a 20% discount actually means for your wallet? You don't always need a calculator. Here are four simple tricks to calculate percentages in your head.

## 1. The 10% Trick
Finding 10% of any number is incredibly easy: just move the decimal point one place to the left. 
- 10% of $45.00 = $4.50
- 10% of $120 = $12.00

## 2. The 5% Trick
Need to find 5%? Just find 10% using the trick above, and cut it in half!
- 5% of $80: 10% is $8. Half of $8 is $4.

## 3. The 20% Trick (The Tipping Rule)
To find 20%—often used for tipping—find 10% and double it.
- 20% of a $65 dinner bill: 10% is $6.50. Double that is $13.00.

## 4. The Reversible Percentage Trick
This is a game-changer: **X% of Y is always equal to Y% of X.**
- What is 4% of 75? That sounds hard.
- But what is 75% of 4? That’s easy: it’s 3.
- Therefore, 4% of 75 is also 3!
